In game

Every screen,in violet glass.

Right Shift opens the Orbit. Pick a tab to open the mod menu, right-click a card for its settings, or move your HUD in Edit HUD. Minecraft's own buttons, lists and text boxes are glass too.

Knives

New

Eight knives held big and close: Karambit, Butterfly, Bayonet, Huntsman, Dagger, Phantom, Katana and Scythe, in Steel, Purple, Crimson, Ice or Gold. Each has its own draw and inspect (press Y). Pick which hotbar slots are knives, or make every sword one.

Skies

New

Ten living skies drawn per pixel: Galaxy, Aether, Nocturne, Amethyst, Opaline, Rain, Snow, Fire, Hollow and Afterglow. Set the speed, brightness, tint, stars and time of day, with light rain, snow or embers to match.

Name Protect Auto

Keys

Four keysto remember.

  • Right Shift

    Orbit and mod menu

    Spin to a tab and open it. Esc in the menu takes you back to the Orbit.

  • C

    Zoom

    Hold to zoom in. Scroll while you hold it to zoom further or back out.

  • Left Alt

    Freelook

    Hold to look around while you keep moving the same way.

  • Y

    Inspect

    Turn your knife over in your hand. Every knife has its own inspect.

Press them now to try. You can change them in Options, Controls, Key Binds, under 3vhy Client.

Install

From downloadto your first match.

  1. Download

    Get 3vhy Client Setup.exe. The button always gives you the newest version.

  2. Run the setup

    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Sign in

    Use the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

  4. Press Play

    Choose 1.21.11 or 26.1 and press Play. In game, press Right Shift.

FAQ

Questions?Short answers.

Is 3vhy Client free?

Yes. The launcher and the mod inside it are free to download and play.

Which Minecraft versions does it run?

Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.1, both on Fabric. The launcher sets up an instance for each with the 3vhy mod already in it.

Do I need a Minecraft account?

Yes. Sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I open the mod menu?

Press Right Shift in game to open the Orbit, then click a tab. In the menu, click a card to switch a mod on or off and right-click it for its settings. You can change the key in Options, Controls, Key Binds.

Can I add other mods?

Yes. Browse Modrinth from the launcher and add mods to an instance. Their buttons turn to glass to match the rest of the game.

What is the Classic installer?

The standard Windows installer, without the 3vhy setup screens. Both install the same launcher.
