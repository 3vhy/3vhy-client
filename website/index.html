The launcher

Pick a version and press Play

3vhy Client sets up Fabric instances for Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.1 with the 3vhy mod already installed. Nothing to drag into a mods folder.

  • Sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition
  • Browse mods, modpacks, shaders and resource packs from Modrinth
  • Change your skin from the Skins page
  • Servers you join show up on the home screen, so you can hop back in with one click
  • Your Discord status shows you're playing 3vhy Client
  • Updates itself: new versions, and the mod inside them, install from the app

The mod

Everything lives behind Right Shift

Right Shift opens the hub. Drag HUD widgets where you want them, click the pencil to change one, or open the mod menu to turn mods on and off. Profiles save a whole setup so you can switch between them.

In game

Three keys to remember

  • Right Shift

    Hub and mod menu

    Move HUD widgets, open their settings, and turn mods on and off.

  • C

    Zoom

    Hold to zoom in. Scroll while you hold it to zoom further or back out.

  • Left Alt

    Freelook

    Hold to look around while you keep moving the same way.

Press them now to try. You can change them in Options, Controls, Key Binds, under 3vhy Client.

Getting started

From download to your first match

  1. Download

    Get 3vhy Client Setup.exe. The button always gives you the newest version.

  2. Run the setup

    The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Sign in

    Use the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

  4. Press Play

    Choose 1.21.11 or 26.1 and press Play. In game, press Right Shift.
